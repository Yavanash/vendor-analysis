# Promotional Sunset Plan

## Objective and target rationale

The primary test population is **Higher-History Current Promo Users**, operationalized with the existing **Promo-Engaged Repeaters** segment. This group contains 497 customers (12.7% of the base), has 100% Observed Promo Exposure, an average Repeat-Purchase History of 31.4, a 2.70 stated purchase-frequency score, 3.81 satisfaction, and $59.44 in Current Observed Spend.

The comparison within current promo users supports this choice. Promo-Exposed Transactors also have 100% Observed Promo Exposure, but have an average Repeat-Purchase History of 16.2, a 1.68 frequency score, 3.54 satisfaction, and $59.24 in Current Observed Spend. The large difference is Repeat-Purchase History, not current basket size. This does not prove that reducing promotions will work; it identifies the more defensible population in which to test it.

## Decision rules

- Define the observation window, minimum sample size, non-inferiority margin, guardrail thresholds, and analysis plan before launch.
- Randomize only among customers eligible for the same campaign, and preserve assignment so exposure is auditable.
- Do not combine Promo-Exposed Transactors with the initial target. Their lower Repeat-Purchase History warrants separate treatment and evidence.
- Read geographic and category results as subgroup checks unless the experiment is powered for those comparisons.
- Do not infer success from Current Observed Spend alone; it is one observed spend measure and is similar across the two current-promo segments.

No margin uplift is estimated here. Contribution margin analysis requires future product cost, fulfillment cost, return cost, and discount amount/depth data.
